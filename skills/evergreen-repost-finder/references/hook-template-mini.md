# hook-template-mini.md

24 fill-in hook templates, 2 per archetype. The full 44-template library ships in the facebook-hook-analyzer skill and the bundle. Archetype definitions and the precedence ladder live in `hook-archetypes.md`.

Version 1.0 | 2026-08-05

---

**How to use these.** Fill the braced slots with subject matter from posts you have actually written. **Keep the structural words:** the negation, the contrast, the punctuation, the rhythm are the pattern; the nouns are yours. A filled template that would work unchanged for any creator in any niche is one to throw away. Each template names the engagement it tends to earn: **comment driver** (asks for something the reader already has an answer to), **share driver** (gives them something to send to one person), **reaction driver** (agreement that costs nothing), **read driver** (earns the click into the body). You can check whether it worked: PostSnag exports Reactions, Comments, and Shares as separate columns, so post, export again, and compute `Comments / (Reactions + Comments + Shares)`.

---

**Question Open**
1. `What would you {action} if {constraint that removes the usual excuse}?` Strongest when the constraint is a deadline or a physical limit. **Comment driver.**
2. `Is it {benign label}, or is it {uncomfortable label}?` The second label is the whole post. **Read driver**, comment driver when the second label stings.

**Bold Claim**
3. `{Thing everyone buys} does not {job they bought it for}. {Thing nobody buys} does.` Second sentence under five words. Earns its keep only if the body proves the swap. **Share driver.**
4. `{Common practice} is the last step, not the first.` Reorders something the reader believes is a starting point. **Reaction driver.**

**Curiosity Gap**
5. `There is one {small specific thing} in every {place} that predicts {the whole outcome}.` The more mundane the thing, the harder the gap pulls. Name it in the second line, never later. **Read driver.**
6. `The cheapest thing in my {kit, stack, process} does the most work, and it is not what you think.` Only use it if your answer is genuinely surprising; a boring reveal costs you the next one. **Read driver.**

**Problem Callout**
7. `Your {thing} is not {assumed problem}. It is {real problem}.` The workhorse. Keep both sentences short. **Reaction driver.**
8. `Your {thing} looks {good} in {favorable condition} and fails on a {unglamorous specific moment}.` "On a Tuesday night" beats "under pressure" every time. **Share driver.**

**Number Promise**
9. `{N} {things} I removed from my {area} that I have not missed once.` Removal lists beat addition lists: addition costs the reader money, removal costs nothing. **Share driver.**
10. `The {N} {items} I tell every {client, reader, new person} to {action} on day one.` "Day one" implies a system behind it. Keep the list to items that need no decision. **Save and share driver.**

**Story Cold Open**
11. `I opened a {person}'s {place} last {weekday} and {specific absurd thing} {happened}.` Name the object exactly. A sled, not "old sports equipment". **Comment driver.**
12. `A {person} {emotional verb} in their own {mundane place} on {weekday}, and it was not about {the obvious subject}.` The "and it was not about" clause turns a story into a question. Answer it in the body. **Share driver**, and the highest-ceiling template here.

**Contrarian Take**
13. `Everyone tells you to {common first step}. That is exactly backwards, and it is why you own {evidence of the failure}.` The evidence clause is what stops it being a hot take. **Comment driver.**
14. `Unpopular opinion: most of us do not have a {named problem}. We have a {real problem} with a {named problem} symptom.` Two words of prefix change the archetype: strip them and it is a Bold Claim. **Comment driver.**

**Direct Address**
15. `{Audience segment}, this one changes nothing about your {constraint they cannot change}.` Leads with the objection the segment always raises, and removes it first. **Save driver.**
16. `If you have {specific tell}, this one is for you.` The tell must be specific enough that people who have it feel caught. **Comment driver.**

**Confession**
17. `I have a {area} in my own {domain} I do not {show or talk about}.` State it flatly. Any softening kills it. **Comment driver.**
18. `I still {do the wrong thing} and I have been doing this for {N years}.` The years number is what makes it land. Without it this is a complaint. **Reaction driver.**

**Command**
19. `Go {small action} right now. Set a timer for {small number} minutes.` The timer is what makes it possible to obey. Without it this is a nag. **Comment driver** if you ask people to report back.
20. `Stop {the visible activity}. Start {the invisible one that matters}.` Under seven words total, or the rhythm is gone. **Reaction driver.**

**Myth Bust**
21. `{Product or method} does not fail because {reason people blame}. It fails because {actual reason}, and no amount of {money or effort} fixes {actual reason}.` The "no amount of" clause stops the reader buying the expensive version of the same mistake. **Save and share driver.**
22. `"{Common belief, stated verbatim}" is not a rule. It is {the real origin of the belief}, repeated until it sounded like one.` Needs a genuine origin story; without one, use a Contrarian Take. **Share driver.**

**Permission Grant**
23. `You are allowed to keep the {ugly or imperfect thing} that works. Nobody is coming to inspect your {domain}.` Names the imagined judge and dismisses them. Do not cut the second sentence. **Share driver.**
24. `You do not have to {the obligation everyone assumes}. {N} of my {clients or readers} never did, and {outcome}.` The count converts permission into evidence. **Reaction driver.**

---

**This will not tell you which template is best.** Your own file knows. Run the archetype analysis, find where your share of the top quartile beats your share of the corpus, then pull from those archetypes. And it will not survive being used as a form: fill a template with a specific object from a post you actually wrote and it disappears into your voice.
